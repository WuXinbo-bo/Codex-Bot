# Expression Upgrade

Production inventory and preview inventory share the same registries. No task content is inferred from a decorative performance.

## Delivery Batches

1. Scheduling: suppress immediate clip repeats and recently overused families; retain task/drag/panel priorities and mask ownership.
2. Emotion and shape vocabulary: twelve emotion families, sixty named poses, six additional morph-compatible silhouettes.
3. Props and performances: five new anchored props, complete take/use/stow sequences; reuse the existing 24 full-face masks and six wearing chains.
4. Integration: state-specific ambient pools and mouse reactions; lifecycle notifications retain their fast panel choreography.
5. Verification: dynamic inventory checks, production reachability checks, finite geometry and browser/native regression tests.

Proposed category percentages are tuning targets, not enforced quotas. Existing mask cooldown and ownership prevent automatic face masks from covering a hand/prop performance. Body and prop anchors remain in the shared renderer.

## Verified Release

- Inventory: 134 expression/pose presets, 142 activity sequences (including 60 short emotional sequences), 18 silhouettes, 24 accessories, 24 existing full-face masks and 6 wearing chains. Emotional poses reuse the existing eye rig; these are not 60 new eye rendering primitives.
- Every new emotion and all 20 prop routines are reachable from production pools. Completion/attention props are restricted to lifecycle pools. Running ambient clips cannot emit terminal effects.
- 181 unit tests passed, including a 30-minute virtual-clock schedule and unique-pose/production-reachability assertions.
- Browser inventory, 1280/390 viewport playback, 1080 emotion/shape path combinations and production panel choreography checks passed. Screenshots are under output/playwright/upgrade-*.png.
- Native smoke passed at 150% scaling with Codex connected, completion retention, settings and window following.
- Release executable: 4,895,744 bytes (4.669 MiB); installer: 1,983,396 bytes (1.892 MiB). Excludes shared WebView2, Codex and runtime memory.

Visual checks sample rendered performances and validate all geometry paths; they do not claim an exhaustive human judgment of every interpolated frame or certification on another physical machine.
